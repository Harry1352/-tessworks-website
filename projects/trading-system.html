THE IDEA

Built around
the problem.

The project brings together market scanning, multi-timeframe analysis, strategy setups, backtesting and risk-aware trade planning. The emphasis is on understanding how a strategy behaves across conditions—not simply generating entries.

Development and evaluation are ongoing. Current demonstrations should be understood as software research and paper-trading workflows, not a live investment service or a promise of returns.
