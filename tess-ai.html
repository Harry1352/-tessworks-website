TESS AI / PROJECT 02

An assistant that
connects ideas to action.

TESS is an evolving AI assistant project exploring conversational interaction, memory and model-routing approaches, with a longer-term ambition to support connected workflows across TessWorks.

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENTAI ASSISTANTEXPERIMENTAL
THE VISION

From conversation
to useful workflows.

The project investigates how an assistant can understand requests, retain useful context, choose suitable model capabilities and eventually coordinate tasks across software tools. The focus is on building a reliable foundation before expanding its autonomy.

Development note: TESS is under development. The wider ecosystem integrations and autonomous capabilities described here are goals, not claims of currently available production features.
