TESS FINTECH / PROJECT 01

Turning market data
into structured decisions.

An algorithmic trading research system being developed to scan cryptocurrency markets, evaluate strategy setups and apply risk-management rules in a controlled paper-trading workflow.

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENTCRYPTO MARKET RESEARCHPAPER-TRADING FOCUS
THE IDEA

Less guesswork.
More structure.

The project explores a systematic workflow: observe market conditions, identify potential setups, define entries and invalidation levels, then evaluate outcomes. The goal is to make strategy testing and risk decisions more consistent—not to promise returns.

Development note: this is a research and paper-trading project. The website does not represent a live execution service, verified profitability or financial advice.
SYSTEM AREAS

Built around a
repeatable process.

01 / OBSERVE

Market scanning

Explore market feeds and multi-timeframe context to identify conditions worth evaluating.

02 / EVALUATE

Strategy logic

Test setup rules, directional filters, confidence scoring and entry/stop/target planning.

03 / PROTECT

Risk framework

Research position sizing, leverage constraints and liquidation-aware risk controls before any live deployment.

DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

Test first.
Validate continuously.

  1. Build and refine strategy rules.
  2. Run historical backtests and inspect trade behaviour.
  3. Evaluate in paper trading before considering any live integration.
  4. Document limitations, risk and performance honestly.
